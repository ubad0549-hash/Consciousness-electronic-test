# Definition of consciousness
  First definition: first-person experience that has qualia.
Personal discussion about the first definition
What is first person and what is qualia? Can we turn these terms into mechanisms that the brain or some form of information processing can explain? Is this definition justified enough to make a mechanistic, step-by-step process for Consciousness?
We can define first-person experience and qualia as information processing. The first-person perspective can be described as a type of information processing directed toward a single center, which directly processes inputs and produces outputs. (personal definition to mechanize it). We can define qualia as a meta-understanding of, and direct involvement in, information processing.
These definitions of first-person and qualia can be somewhat descriptive of brain processes and different informational systems.
I would argue that these definitions are justified when describing mechanisms, but I believe there need to be stricter bounds on what qualifies as first-person and qualia.
